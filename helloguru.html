The Kitchen Discipline

Why Rayalaseema Cooking Cannot Be Rushed

The rugged hinterlands of Rayalaseema demand culinary resilience. The cuisine balances high heat from sun-cured Kurnool red chillies with the soothing density of finger millet (Ragi) and rich A2 cultured butter.

Unlike modern gas stovetops, our deghs sit over slow tamarind and babool coals. The aroma of slow-charred country chicken broth and roasted ground spices is woven directly into every grain of rice.

"If the garlic is not crushed with mortar and pestle right before the ghee hits the pan, it is simply not Rayalaseema Erra Karam."

Woodfire Dhum Sealing

Pounded dough locks the steam within heavy hand-beaten copper pots, ensuring tender braised cuts.

Kurnool Erra Karam

Stone-ground Kadapa chillies, whole garlic pods, roasted cumin and sea salt ground fresh every dawn.

Zero Commercial Powders

We reject artificial coloring agents and bottled essences. Rich red hues stem purely from sun-dried chillies.

Chikmagalur Filter Kaapi

Served in authentic brass davarah glasses with thick farm milk and first-drip chicory decoction.

Service Timeline

Four-Phase Kitchen Schedule

To maintain strict freshness, our firewood stoves transition through four distinct preparation cycles daily.

Phase 01

Morning Tiffins

06:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Strictly dedicated to morning breakfast specials: Uggani Bajji, Ghee Erra Karam Dosa, Pesarattu Upma, and Thatte Idlis.

Phase 02

Afternoon Lunch

11:00 AM – 05:00 PM

Full feast menu: Rayalaseema Kodi Dhum Biryani, Mutton Biryani, Ragi Sangati with Natukodi Pulusu & Royal Meals.

Phase 03

Tiffin & Dinner

05:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Both evening Tiffins (Dosas, Uggani) and full Dinner feasts (Biryanis, Parotta Salna, and Curries) are active simultaneously.

Phase 04

Kitchen Closed

11:00 PM – 06:00 AM

Stoves rest for overnight coal banking. Digital menu browsing is enabled, with online ordering reopening at 06:00 AM.
